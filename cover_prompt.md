Title: The Weeknight Dutch Oven

Subtitle: 85 One-Pot Dinners for Busy Families

Author: Mara J. Ellison

## Reference observations

- ref1.jpg uses an oversized high-contrast title in the upper zone and a bright overhead food hero that dominates the lower half.
- ref2.jpg uses extremely bold thumbnail-readable typography, saturated food photography, and strong dark-to-bright color contrast.
- ref3.jpg uses a stacked upper title block, a warm kitchen setting, and one unmistakable cooking vessel as the central hero.

## Shared winning pattern

Use a strong upper title zone with simple, high-contrast hierarchy, then devote the lower half to one abundant, photorealistic food hero. Make the cooking vessel immediately recognizable, keep the food warm and appetizing, use a restrained contrasting palette, and preserve enough calm negative space for large deterministic cover typography. The mood should feel practical, welcoming, modern, and family-centered. The design must be original and must not copy, trace, or closely imitate any reference.

## Front-art generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: text-free portrait front-cover artwork for a 6 x 9 cookbook
Book metadata for layout context only: exact title "The Weeknight Dutch Oven"; exact subtitle "85 One-Pot Dinners for Busy Families"; exact author "Mara J. Ellison". Do not render any of these strings in the artwork.
Primary request: Create original premium cookbook cover photography centered on a classic enameled Dutch oven filled with a colorful tomato-braised chicken and vegetable dinner, with golden chicken pieces, carrots, potatoes, green beans, tomatoes, and fresh herbs.
Scene/backdrop: Warm contemporary family kitchen table, subtle linen napkin and wood texture, clean editorial styling.
Subject: One unmistakable deep Dutch oven as the sole hero vessel, lid resting nearby, abundant wholesome one-pot dinner visible from a slightly elevated three-quarter viewpoint.
Style/medium: Photorealistic professional food photography, natural textures, believable steam, crisp appetizing detail, premium bookstore quality.
Composition/framing: PORTRAIT orientation, 2:3 aspect ratio. Reserve the upper 40 percent as calm, dark midnight-indigo negative space with soft kitchen atmosphere for later title typography. Place the Dutch oven across the lower 55 percent, centered and large, with safe breathing room near every edge. No border and no barcode zone on the front.
Lighting/mood: Warm side light, inviting weeknight comfort, family-friendly, confident and practical.
Color palette: Midnight indigo background, copper Dutch oven, tomato red, carrot gold, herb green, warm cream highlights.
Materials/textures: Glossy enamel, real cooked food texture, linen weave, natural wood grain, gentle steam.
Constraints: Portrait artwork only. No text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, badges, banners, watermarks, icons, vector art, clip art, people, hands, violence, or blood. Do not imitate any reference cover. Keep the upper title area visually quiet and high contrast.
Avoid: Extra cookware, clutter, artificial food styling, excessive garnish, warped utensils, duplicate ingredients, illegible pseudo-text.
No pork, no pig imagery, no alcohol, no bottles or glasses of wine or beer. Wholesome and family-safe.
