# Trademark Screen - required before publishing (works even if Claude is unavailable)​‍‍​‌‍‌‌‍‍‌‌‍‌‌‌‍‍‍‍‍‌‍‍‍‌‍‌‌‌‌‍‌‌‍‌​

No book may be published until its TITLE has passed a trademark screen. This is a **preliminary
risk screen, not legal clearance** - you (the operator) are responsible, and every Publish is
confirmed by you (global hard rule #8).

When Claude Code is available it runs this screen automatically at Phase 0. If Claude is
**unavailable** (usage limit / outage) and you still need to move a book toward publishing, run
the screen yourself in **ChatGPT** (which has web browsing), then publish only on a GREEN result.

## Paste this into ChatGPT (with browsing on)

Act as a KDP trademark and market risk screener. This is a PRELIMINARY risk screen, not legal
clearance. For the book title below, using live web search:
  TITLE: "<your title>"
  MARKETPLACE: Amazon US (amazon.com)
1) TRADEMARK: search the exact title + distinctive words on the USPTO trademark database
   (tmsearch.uspto.gov) plus Google for registered/pending marks in books/publishing or related
   classes. Report GREEN (clear), YELLOW (caution, explain) or RED (high risk - a registered mark
   or famous brand) with the specific marks found.
2) MARKET: search the exact title and close variants on Amazon. List the top 5-10 competing books
   (title, author, approx. reviews if visible), note if an identical title already exists, and give
   a STRONG / WEAK / SATURATED verdict with evidence.
3) If YELLOW/RED, suggest 3-5 safer title variants that keep the seed words.
End with one line exactly: VERDICT: <GREEN|YELLOW|RED> - <one-sentence reason>.

## Decision rule

- **GREEN** -> ok to proceed toward publishing (with your final Publish confirmation).
- **YELLOW** -> proceed only after you understand and accept the specific concern.
- **RED** -> do NOT publish; choose a safer title (keep the seed words).

**Never publish a title that is UNVERIFIED or RED.** Screen first, then publish.
