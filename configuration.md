## Locked Production Choices

- Exact title: Tacoma User Guide
- Covered version: 2026 Toyota Tacoma, United States specification
- Audience: all-level owners, with first-time truck owners prioritized
- Use context: personal ownership, daily driving, hauling, towing, and off-road preparation
- Page band: standard, 180 to 250 pages
- Trim: 8.5 x 11 inches
- Chapters: 14
- Body type: Times New Roman 11 pt with 1.15 line spacing
- Content path: official Toyota documentation plus current primary-source research
- Visual mode: heavy visual, with labeled component diagrams and procedure visuals

## Design Fingerprint

- Palette: deep evergreen #174F45, signal amber #C5892F, steel gray #505A5F,
  pale sage #E7F0EC, and alert red #A6382D
- Chapter openers: left-aligned chapter number in amber, evergreen task title,
  steel-gray route line, and a full-width shaded outcome statement
- Callout styles: amber CAUTION rail, red WARNING rail, evergreen NOTICE panel,
  and steel VERSION NOTE panel
- Divider motif: paired tire-track bars, expressed as simple parallel rules
- Tables: evergreen header with white text and alternating pale-sage rows
- Folios: outside-corner page numbers in evergreen with chapter task in steel gray
- Voice: concise field instructor, calm, exact, and non-promotional
- Pattern: prepare, perform, verify, recover

## Version Declaration

This book covers the 2026 Toyota Tacoma for the United States market as documented
through August 11, 2026. Equipment varies by trim, package, powertrain, cab, bed,
production date, and software version. Vehicle labels and the Owner's Manual supplied
for the specific truck control whenever they differ from this guide.
