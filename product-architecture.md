# Product Architecture Map​‍‍​‌‍‌‌‍‍‌‌‍‌‌‌‍‍‍‍‍‌‍‍‍‌‍‌‌‌‌‍‌‌‍‌​

## Vehicle Identity Layer

- Model year, cab, bed length, trim, powertrain, and drive configuration
- Vehicle identification number, tire and loading label, certification label
- Equipment status: standard, optional, package-dependent, or not equipped

## Physical Control Layer

- Smart key and mechanical key
- Doors, tailgate, windows, hood, fuel door, and bed power points
- Driver's seat, steering wheel, mirrors, pedals, parking brake, and shifter
- Engine switch, meter control switches, lighting stalk, and wiper stalk
- Climate, multimedia, camera, drive-mode, four-wheel-drive, and off-road controls

## Information Layer

- Multi-information display
- Warning lights and messages
- Toyota Audio Multimedia display
- Toyota App and connected-services status
- Camera views, parking support, and trailer information

## Driving Layer

- Starting, shifting, braking, steering, visibility, and parking
- Toyota Safety Sense 3.0 systems and software-version checks
- Two-wheel drive, part-time four-wheel drive, and full-time four-wheel drive where equipped
- Drive modes, traction systems, off-road systems, and stabilizer controls where equipped
- Payload, cargo securement, trailer setup, and towing aids where equipped

## Ownership Layer

- Fuel and charging considerations by powertrain
- Tires, fluids, filters, lights, wipers, battery, and scheduled service
- Cleaning, corrosion prevention, accessory compatibility, and software updates
- Warning response, jump starting, flat tire response, recovery, and professional towing

## Terminology Master

Use the official 2026 labels for every control and system. Key names include Toyota
Safety Sense 3.0, PCS (Pre-Collision System), LTA (Lane Tracing Assist), LDA (Lane
Departure Alert), PDA (Proactive Driving Assist), RSA (Road Sign Assist), Dynamic
Radar Cruise Control, Emergency Driving Stop System, Toyota App, i-FORCE, i-FORCE MAX,
TRD Pro, Trailhunter, and IsoDynamic Performance Front Seats. Use "if equipped" every
time availability varies.
