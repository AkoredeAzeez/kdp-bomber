Genie package version: 5.5 (master, 2026-08-14)

Highlights since 5.3:
- Chapter-by-chapter build law: one chapter at a time, delivered as BOTH a
  DOCX and a PDF preview before the next begins, in full premium color.
- Page numbering starts at the Introduction (page 1); the Table of Contents
  is reworked after the whole book is finished and verified in the final PDF.
- 8.5x11 books: block-letter chapter titles 28pt+, subheads 14-18pt, nothing
  ever cut off; introductions fill their pages.
- Cover and A+ production: Codex (native gpt-image-2) is the default engine;
  block-letter titles, photoreal comprehensive prompts, dense-infographic A+
  (one module per 970x600 file); reference databases self-build on install.
- Travel guides: 8.5x11, minimum 4 original maps per guide.
- Per-niche house formatting standards for cookbooks, textbooks and
  educational books, travel guides, workbooks, craft and how-to, self-help.
- Custom formatting: save your own style per niche with
  "Genie, customize formatting for [niche]"; it survives updates.
- Clean in-place updater (genie_update.py): update without reinstalling and
  without losing your books, projects, or databases.
- First-run bootstrap installs all dependencies automatically on Claude Code,
  Codex CLI, and Codex in VS Code.
