### The Genie quick-start guide

Welcome! Genie is your complete book production studio: it researches,
writes, formats, and illustrates professional books for Amazon KDP and
beyond. This guide takes you from a blank screen to a finished, upload-ready
book in about two days. Keep it next to you for your first run; after that
you will not need it.

---

## Before you start (10 minutes, once)

1. You have already installed Genie (if you can talk to it,
   you are done with setup).
2. Open your terminal or VS Code in the Genie folder and start your engine
   (Claude Code or Codex).
3. Type: **"Genie, standup"** and press Enter. Genie introduces itself and
   reports its status. First run on a new computer takes a few extra
   minutes while Genie finishes preparing its reference libraries; it will
   tell you what it is doing.

That is the whole setup. Everything else in this guide is one message at a
time.

---

## Hour 0 to 1: choose your book

You need exactly one thing to start: a book idea. Three ways to get one:

- **You already know.** A cookbook for a specific diet, a study guide, a
  travel guide, a children's book: if you have the idea, skip ahead.
- **Ask Genie for ideas**: say
  *"Genie, find me 5 promising niches in [area I care about]"* and Genie
  researches live Amazon data: what sells, what is crowded, where the gaps
  are.
- **Validate your idea first**: say
  *"Genie, validate this niche: [your idea]"* before committing.

**Title tips:** say what the book is and who it is for, in the words a
buyer would type into Amazon. "High Protein Cottage Cheese Cookbook" beats
"Culinary Adventures with Curds." Genie will automatically check your
title for trademark conflicts before any writing starts, and will warn you
honestly if the title is risky.

---

## Hour 1: the one message that starts everything

Send Genie a single message with your book brief. Template:

> Genie, write this book:
> **Title:** [your exact title]
> **Marketplace:** [Amazon.com / Amazon.co.uk / ...]
> **Language:** [English (US) / ...]
> **Pages per chapter:** [e.g. 8]
> **Maximum pages:** [e.g. 120]
> **Special instructions:** [anything non-negotiable: audience, must-have
> topics, things to avoid]

A real example:

> Genie, write this book:
> Title: Mediterranean Diet Cookbook for Busy Families
> Marketplace: Amazon.com
> Language: English (US)
> Pages per chapter: 8
> Maximum pages: 130
> Special instructions: audience is working parents, every recipe under
> 30 minutes, include a 14-day meal plan.

That is it. Genie takes it from here.

---

## Hours 1 to 36: what happens automatically

Genie now runs the full production line. You will see it:

1. **Clear the title** (trademark screen; risky titles are stopped with
   safer alternatives offered).
2. **Research the market**: competitors, pricing, keywords.
3. **Design the book**: outline, chapter plan, and a unique interior
   design; no two Genie books look alike.
4. **Write chapter by chapter.** After EVERY chapter you receive the
   finished chapter as both a **Word file and a PDF**, exactly as it will
   print, full color formatting included.

**Your only job during this phase:** skim each chapter preview. If
something is off, say so in plain words: *"Genie, chapter 3: make the tone
warmer and fix the second recipe's servings."* Genie corrects it and
re-delivers before moving on.

**Want it hands-free?** Say **"Genie, run on autopilot"** and it will
proceed chapter to chapter without waiting, still delivering every preview.

**Want your own look?** Say **"Genie, customize formatting for
[cookbooks/travel guides/...]"** and answer its questions once; every
future book in that category uses your style.

---

## Hours 36 to 48: finishing touches

When the last chapter passes quality control, Genie automatically:

- assembles the complete manuscript (Word + print-ready PDF, checked page
  by page against print margins);
- reworks the table of contents so every page number is exact;
- generates interior images where the content needs them;
- **PRO/MAX:** produces your cover (front, back, spine, and the full
  print wrap, measured on Amazon's official calculator) and A+ content
  images for your product page;
- **PRO/MAX:** writes your complete KDP metadata package: description,
  the 7 backend keywords, and category picks, delivered as its own
  document next to the manuscript.

---

## Publishing

- **Publishing:** say *"Genie, publish [Title] to KDP."* With you logged in
  to your KDP account, Genie fills in everything from the metadata
  package and shows you a final summary. **You always make the final
  Publish click yourself**, and you always enter your own account, bank,
  and tax details; Genie never touches those.
- **Starter and PRO:** upload manually at kdp.amazon.com: create a new
  title, paste the metadata Genie gave you, upload the manuscript PDF and
  cover file, order a proof if you want one, and publish. It takes about
  20 minutes.

---

## Good habits and troubleshooting

- **Resuming:** if a session ends mid-book, start a new one and say
  *"Genie, resume [Title]"*. Nothing is lost; Genie saves after every
  chapter.
- **Daily driving:** *"Genie, standup"* each morning shows status,
  anything waiting on you, and what is planned.
- **Updates:** when a new Community Edition zip is released, run
  `python genie_update.py <zip>` in your Genie folder. Your books and
  your settings all survive updates.
- **Something looks wrong in a preview?** Tell Genie in plain language,
  chapter and problem. It fixes and re-delivers. You never need technical
  terms.
