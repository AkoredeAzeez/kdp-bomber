## Verdict

**CAUTION, approved to continue.**

"Tacoma" identifies Toyota's pickup model in this book context. The phrase is used
only to identify the product covered by an independent user guide. The exact title
does not claim official status, authorization, sponsorship, or endorsement.

## Required Controls

- Preserve the exact title, Tacoma User Guide.
- State clearly that the book is independent and unofficial.
- Do not use Toyota or Tacoma logos, badges, proprietary type treatments, or trade dress.
- Use product names only as needed to identify compatible vehicles, systems, and manuals.
- Identify 2026 model-year coverage prominently on the title page, cover brief,
  Introduction, description, and metadata.
- Do not imply that the author or book is approved by Toyota.

## Preliminary Registry Evidence

- USPTO guidance requires exact wording and expanded similarity searches.
- Public trademark records associate TOYOTA TACOMA with Toyota Jidosha Kabushiki Kaisha.
- Toyota's current official materials use Tacoma as the model name for its 2026 pickup.

This is a preliminary publishing-risk screen, not legal clearance. The final cover and
metadata require a pre-publication review for nominative-use framing.

## Operator Decision

The operator instructed Genie to continue after the 2026 current-edition scope was
announced. The CAUTION verdict is recorded as approved for production, with the controls
above remaining mandatory.
