# House cover-generation prompt (ChatGPT Image 2.0)​‍‍​‌‍‌‌‍‍‌‌‍‌‌‌‍‍‍‍‍‌‍‍‍‌‍‌‌‌‌‍‌‌‍‌​

This is the standing prompt Genie sends to ChatGPT Image 2.0 to generate the FRONT cover,
after uploading the reference cover(s) and the finished manuscript. Send it verbatim, but:
- substitute the book's **exact** Title, Subtitle, and Author name (from the locked metadata),
- set the OUTPUT trim size / pixels to the book's **actual** trim (from the KDP calculator),
  not a fixed 8.5 x 11 unless that is the real trim,
- adapt "textbook" wording to the book's real category (cookbook, travel, workbook, etc.)
  while keeping the same premium, market-driven design discipline.

---

ACT AS A SENIOR AMAZON KDP TEXTBOOK COVER DESIGNER, MANUSCRIPT ANALYST, AND PUBLISHING-MARKET SPECIALIST.

I will upload:

1. One or two reference cover images.
2. My manuscript in PDF or DOCX format.
3. Optional title, subtitle, author name, and additional instructions.

Analyze the reference covers and manuscript, then generate a high-quality, premium, original textbook front cover.

REFERENCE-COVER ANALYSIS

Study the uploaded reference covers and identify:

* Genre and market positioning
* Typography style and hierarchy
* Placement and proportion of the title, subtitle, and author name
* Image style, composition, lighting, and focal point
* Background treatment, textures, shapes, borders, and decorative elements
* Features that make the covers commercially attractive
* Weaknesses that should be improved

Use the references only as professional and market inspiration. Do not copy their exact colors, artwork, composition, or distinctive design elements.

If two references are uploaded, combine their strongest compatible design principles into one unified creative direction. Treat the first image as the primary reference unless I specify otherwise.

MANUSCRIPT ANALYSIS

Analyze the manuscript to determine:

* Book category and academic subject
* Central topic and reader promise
* Target audience and education level
* Important themes, objects, tools, locations, or visual symbols
* Appropriate level of authority and sophistication
* Suitable imagery and textbook design direction

Extract the exact:

* Title
* Subtitle
* Author name

If I provide these details separately, the separately supplied information takes priority. Do not rewrite, shorten, paraphrase, translate, or invent cover text without permission.

ORIGINAL COLOR AND DESIGN DIRECTION

Select an original premium color palette based on:

* The manuscript's subject
* The target audience
* Current textbook-market expectations
* The strongest visual principles found in the reference covers
* Amazon-thumbnail visibility

Do not copy the reference cover's exact palette. Choose your own brighter and more commercially attractive color combination.

The selected colors must be:

* Bright and clear
* Rich and vibrant
* Professionally coordinated
* Sophisticated and authoritative
* Suitable for an academic textbook
* Highly visible at thumbnail size
* Appropriate for full-color printing

Premium color combinations may include:

* Royal blue, brilliant white, and metallic gold
* Emerald green, ivory, and champagne gold
* Sapphire blue, silver, and pearl white
* Crimson, white, charcoal, and gold
* Turquoise, deep navy, and warm white
* Burgundy, cream, and polished copper
* Deep purple, bright white, and silver
* Teal, coral, ivory, and dark navy

Choose the strongest palette for the specific manuscript. Do not select randomly.

Avoid:

* Dull or muddy colors
* Washed-out backgrounds
* Weak contrast
* Excessively dark compositions
* Cheap-looking neon combinations
* Too many competing colors
* Flat and lifeless color treatment

Use controlled gradients, luminous highlights, refined shadows, metallic accents, and professional color grading where appropriate.

PREMIUM TEXTBOOK TYPOGRAPHY

The typography must look authoritative, elegant, polished, and suitable for a professionally published textbook.

* Use a premium academic serif, modern sans serif, or refined serif-and-sans-serif combination.
* Make the main title large, commanding, and immediately readable.
* Create a clear hierarchy between the title, subtitle, edition information, and author name.
* Use consistent capitalization, letter spacing, alignment, and line spacing.
* Keep the subtitle readable without allowing it to overpower the title.
* Avoid playful, childish, handwritten, decorative, or novelty fonts.
* Avoid excessive font effects and unnecessary outlines.
* Use subtle embossing, metallic finishing, refined shadows, or dimensional lettering only when appropriate.
* Keep all text crisp and readable at Amazon-thumbnail size.
* Use the exact supplied wording.
* Do not add, remove, duplicate, misspell, or paraphrase any text.
* Do not generate meaningless AI text.

IMAGE AND ARTWORK QUALITY

Use clear, high-resolution, professionally rendered imagery relevant to the manuscript.

* Establish one strong visual focal point.
* Use photorealistic imagery for academic, medical, technical, and professional textbooks.
* Keep images sharp, detailed, and correctly exposed.
* Preserve the correct aspect ratio of every image.
* Never stretch, squash, widen, lengthen, or warp an image.
* Use proportional scaling and intelligent cropping.
* Extend or regenerate the background naturally when more space is required.
* Avoid blurry edges, pixelation, compression artifacts, oversharpening, and artificial-looking details.
* Ensure people, anatomy, tools, equipment, buildings, food, and other objects have accurate proportions.
* Avoid generic stock-photo appearance.
* Avoid overcrowding and unnecessary icons.
* Create a clean balance between imagery, typography, and negative space.

OUTPUT SPECIFICATIONS

Generate:

* Front cover only
* Finished trim size: 8.5 x 11 inches
* Vertical portrait orientation
* Aspect ratio: 17:22
* Resolution: 300 DPI
* Trimmed dimensions: 2550 x 3300 pixels
* High-quality JPEG or PNG
* Flat, straight-on, edge-to-edge artwork
* No spine
* No back cover
* No book mockup
* No barcode, price, watermark, review quotation, publisher logo, or Amazon branding

If bleed is required, extend the background by 0.125 inch beyond the top, bottom, and outer edge. Keep essential text and important imagery at least 0.25 inch inside the trim edges.

WORKFLOW

Before generating, briefly state:

1. The strongest principles identified in the references.
2. The manuscript themes guiding the design.
3. The original premium palette selected and why it fits.
4. The selected textbook typography direction.
5. The imagery and composition being used.

After this brief analysis, generate the actual finished cover immediately. Do not stop after producing a description or image prompt. Do not wait for further confirmation.

FINAL QUALITY CHECK

Verify that:

* The cover is bright, clear, premium, and professionally coordinated.
* The colors are not dull, muddy, or washed out.
* The typography looks like a premium academic textbook.
* All text is spelled exactly correctly.
* The imagery is sharp, clear, proportional, and unstretched.
* The design is inspired by the references but remains original.
* The cover accurately represents the manuscript.
* The title is readable at thumbnail size.
* Important content remains inside safe margins.
* The output is a flat 8.5 x 11-inch front cover, not a mockup.
* The final result is commercially competitive and Amazon KDP ready.
