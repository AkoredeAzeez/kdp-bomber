---
name: uapf-docx-assembly
description: Build the rolling Word manuscript with professional formatting, new-page chapter starts, a 2–3 page Introduction, and references confined to back matter.
---

1. Read the locked format profile, Master formatting rules, page architecture,
   introduction brief, and autopilot requirements.
2. Assemble front matter, Introduction, chapters in numeric order, and back matter.
3. Use Times New Roman throughout by default, 12-point body text unless an
   approved exception applies, 0.7-inch margins, justified prose, left-aligned
   lists/tables/headings, and centered chapter titles.
4. Start every chapter on a new page.
4b. Apply the GLOBAL premium interior mandate from AGENTS.md ("Manuscript
    formatting — premium interior") to every book in every niche: styled
    chapter openers (colored labels, title in principal color, colored rule or
    motif), colored typographic system (subheadings, glyphs, folios), tinted
    callout panels with colored borders and bold lead-in words (2+ styles per
    book), styled tables (shaded headers, row tints), shaded header bars on
    recurring units, and white space discipline — all in the book's unique
    fingerprint palette, all grayscale-legible. Plain black-on-white
    formatting is an assembly failure.
5. Render and inspect the Introduction; adjust until it occupies approximately
   2–3 pages.
6. Exclude all raw `SRC-####` identifiers from the assembled manuscript.
7. Build the references section in back matter without internal IDs.
8. Keep every image and table fully inside the page margins (the live text
   area): scale down anything that would overflow the left, right, top, or
   bottom margin, and never let a table column or an image bleed into or past a
   margin. Reduce, wrap, or shrink a wide table before it touches a margin.
9. Insert only real image files and retain their prompt files.
9b. Caption every image directly beneath it as "Figure C.N: description", and
    label every table directly beneath it as "Table C.N" (optional short title
    after the number). Number figures and tables per chapter and reset the
    sequence at each new chapter; style captions and labels in the caption style.
10. Save to `build/working-manuscript.docx`, validate, and record the result.
