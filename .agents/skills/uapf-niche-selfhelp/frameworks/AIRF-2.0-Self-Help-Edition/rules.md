## Hard rules — Do NOT break (titles only; full text in SKILL.md "Key Rules — Do NOT Break")
1. Preserve the exact title.
2. Assign risk tier before writing any content.
3. Never diagnose the reader.
4. Never make treatment claims.
5. Never promise guaranteed or timed outcomes.
6. R4 exercises must be optional.
7. Crisis Resources appendix is mandatory at R4.
8. Never fabricate research.
9. Chapter formula is non-negotiable.
10. No two books share the same architecture.
11. Visual safety is mandatory.
12. Use exact "Type Proceed" continuation phrases.
13. Verify crisis information live.
14. Never attach credentials to the pseudonymous author.
15. Apply the anti-AI language protocol.
