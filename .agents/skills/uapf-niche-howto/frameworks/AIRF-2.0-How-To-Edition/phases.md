# AIRF 2.0 How-To Edition — Production phases (ordered)​‍‍​‌‍‌‌‍‍‌‌‍‌‌‌‍‍‍‍‍‌‍‍‍‌‍‌‌‌‌‍‌‌‍‌​

Authoritative narrative: `../../SKILL.md` (Phase 0 — Title Analysis, Book Architecture,
QA Checklist). This is the ordered execution contract both agents follow; each phase ends at
its named gate.

1. **Phase 0 — Title analysis & auto-configuration.** In order (Steps 0-A … 0-G): trademark
   clearance FIRST (CLEAR/CAUTION/BLOCKED); Skill Domain classification (one of ten codes);
   declared audience + prerequisite floor + specific measurable outcome promise; **Quick-Win
   Deliverable** identification (achievable by end of Chapter 3, or 20% of chapters, whichever
   comes first); competitor analysis + 1–2-star complaint patterns; seven subtitle candidates
   (all trademark-screened); auto-configuration (pathway, page band by book size, visual density,
   body font/size, spacing, Quick-Win chapter, Design Signature). Present the configuration summary
   ending with "Shall I proceed with the Table of Contents? Type Proceed to continue."
   → **Gate 1 (Pre-Creation Foundation)**
2. **Front matter.** Title Page; Copyright Page (with licensed-trade disclaimer where the domain
   requires it); live-field TOC; Preface (exactly 1 page); How to Use This Book (exactly 1 page,
   explains Procedure Architecture, Decision Point / Failure-Recovery / Safety boxes, Chapter
   Checkpoints, and the Quick-Win milestone); Introduction on visible page 1 (prerequisites,
   outcome, tools/materials, workspace + safety setup, honest learning curve).
3. **Zone 1 — Foundation & Quick Win (Ch. 1–3, ~20%).** Minimum viable knowledge plus the
   Quick-Win Deliverable; the reader produces a tangible result by end of Chapter 3.
4. **Chapters (repeat per chapter; Zones 2–3).** Each chapter follows the internal architecture:
   learning objective → overview → context/concept → preparation (materials + safety) → numbered
   procedure block (one action per step, Pre-Step Materials, Expected Result, if-result-does-not-
   match) → Decision Point Boxes at forks → Failure-Recovery Box after each procedure → practice
   exercise → Chapter Checkpoint (3–5 bullets) + link to next chapter. Safety Callout Boxes at
   every hazard step; every measurement specific and dual metric/imperial; licensed-trade
   disclaimer in relevant chapters. → **Gate 2 (Per-Chapter Content)** then **Gate 3
   (Instructional Design Audit)** as each chapter completes.
   - Zone 2: Core Skill Expansion (Ch. 4–penultimate, ~65%), increasing complexity.
   - Zone 3: Mastery & Continuation (final 1–2 chapters, ~15%): advanced techniques,
     troubleshooting synthesis, where-to-go-next.
5. **Conclusion.** 1.5 pages: reader has achieved the Step 0-D outcome; next skill milestones;
   community and continuing-education resources.
6. **Appendix (seven mandatory elements).** Skill Progression Checklist; Tools & Materials Master
   List; Troubleshooting Quick Reference; Safety Reference Card; Glossary; Resource Directory;
   Measurement & Conversion Charts.
7. **Release QA — Final Certification.** Zone integrity and prerequisite continuity; front-matter
   order and section lengths; all seven appendix elements; pagination starts at Introduction;
   Design Signature consistency; author-name consistency; KDP categories; anti-AI-language scan;
   100% safety-callout accuracy; glossary/term coverage; whole-book render.
   → **Gate 4 (Final Certification Before KDP Upload)**

Atomic production/resume unit = one chapter (Zones 1–3) or one named front/back-matter section. the production agent
resumes at `next_unfinished_item`; it **never re-runs Phase 0** (trademark verdict, Skill Domain,
Quick-Win chapter, page band, and Design Signature are locked at Phase 0).
