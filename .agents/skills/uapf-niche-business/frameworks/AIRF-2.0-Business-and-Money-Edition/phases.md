# AIRF 2.0 Business and Money Edition — Production phases (ordered)​‍‍​‌‍‌‌‍‍‌‌‍‌‌‌‍‍‍‍‍‌‍‍‍‌‍‌‌‌‌‍‌‌‍‌​

Authoritative narrative: `../../SKILL.md` (Phase 0, Book Architecture, QA Checklist). This is the
ordered execution contract the production agent follow; each phase ends at the named gate.
**R3 verification is mandatory at every phase — it is never downgraded.**

1. **Phase 0 — Title analysis & auto-configuration.** Title intake (verbatim law); language and
   marketplace; tax/legal jurisdiction; elevated trademark screen (GREEN/YELLOW/RED); subject
   analysis and category tier (+ optional secondary); confirm R3 active; year-stamped vs evergreen
   edition decision; five subtitles post-clearance (no outcome promise); pseudonym
   (First Name M. Surname, no credentials); typography, reference style, visual strategy;
   competitor research (10-20 print comparables → mean/median/min/max/central range, median-preferred
   page target). Stamp the canonical classification line into `book_lock.md` with
   `framework=AIRF 2.0 Business and Money Edition`. → **Gate 1 (Configuration)**
2. **Structure & front matter.** Unique TOC / learning journey for the tier; page allocation sums
   to the competitor-informed target; front matter in exact order with tier-matched disclaimer and
   (where relevant) investment-risk statement; clean copyright page; Word skeleton
   (6x9, 0.7in margins, Times New Roman, live TOC, Introduction = visible page 1). → **Gate 2 (Structure)**
3. **Chapters (repeat per chapter).** Adaptive Chapter Formula; worked numerical examples with
   assumptions stated and independently recomputed (losing/break-even where honest); dynamic facts
   verified live, as-of-dated, jurisdiction-labeled, and logged; options-not-recommendations framing;
   referral/out-of-scope routing where triggered; real-company/living-person protocol; compulsory
   image rule honored for the page projection; voice audit (no filler, no em dashes). → **Gate 3 (Per-Chapter)**
4. **Back matter.** Conclusion about 1.5 formatted pages (realistic expectations, no new major
   method); appendices of practical value including the rates-and-limits appendix concentrating
   volatile figures; references in the selected style with all links verified.
5. **Release QA.** Title verbatim everywhere including metadata; full Dynamic-Facts Verification Log
   re-checked; book-wide worked-example re-verification; one convention throughout; earnings-claims
   compliance across manuscript/subtitle/description/imagery; whole-book render; uniqueness audit;
   AI-image disclosure. → **Gate 4 (Release)**

Atomic production/resume unit = one chapter (Phase 3) or one named front/back-matter section. the production agent
resumes at `next_unfinished_item`; it never re-runs Phase 0 (classification, tier, R3, and the
locked title are fixed).
