## Hard rules — Do NOT break (titles only; full text in SKILL.md "Key Rules — Do NOT Break")
1. R3 is mandatory for every OV-BIZ title.
2. No return promises, income claims, or guaranteed outcomes anywhere.
3. Math audit every worked example.
4. Jurisdiction-label and "as of"-date every tax, legal, and program fact; log it; teach the lookup.
5. The not-professional-advice disclaimer is required in every book, tier-matched.
6. Preserve the supplied title verbatim, character for character, everywhere.
7. Options, never personal recommendations; no urgency or pressure; route crises to professionals.
8. Never fabricate credentials, sources, data, results, ISBNs, publishers, or quotes.
9. Content standards are absolute (no alcohol/pork/gambling/adult/predatory-lending, no MLM/pyramid).
10. Real companies and living persons: public record only, attribution discipline, no invented scenes.
11. Charts tell the truth: grayscale-legible, zero-based or clearly labeled axes.
12. No em dashes; body justified; lists/tables left aligned; 6x9, 0.7in margins, live TOC, Intro = page 1.
13. Uniqueness audit before delivery, including against sibling series volumes.
14. Trademark screen before subtitles, elevated for financial brands and certification marks.
15. Every title is built as an annually refreshable edition (volatile figures bounded + lookup method).

**Deterministic checks** for the computable rules (competitor page-target statistics, median-preferred
target, compulsory image-rule threshold, page-band membership) are implemented in `genie_business.py`;
validation gates in `validation.json`.
