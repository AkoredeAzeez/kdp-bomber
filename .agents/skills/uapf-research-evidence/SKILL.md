---
name: uapf-research-evidence
description: Build chapter research packets, source registers, and claim ledgers under UAPF Phase II and III. Use before drafting factual content or when evidence is missing.
---

1. Read the locked scope, routed overlay, risk regime, chapter brief, and Master
   research rules.
2. Choose Option 1, 2, or 3 and document why.
3. Prefer current primary and authoritative sources matched to the marketplace.
4. Record every consulted source with a stable ID and access date.
5. Record every material claim, claim class, source IDs, currency date, and
   verification status.
6. Apply the required two-source floor. Document justified exceptions.
7. Record contradictory evidence and resolve it honestly or remove/qualify the
   claim.
8. Do not draft polished chapter prose until the research-depth gate passes.
9. For high-risk content, identify required qualified human review.
