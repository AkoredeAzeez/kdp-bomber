# AIRF 2.0 Travel Edition — Production phases (ordered)​‍‍​‌‍‌‌‍‍‌‌‍‌‌‌‍‍‍‍‍‌‍‍‍‌‍‌‌‌‌‍‌‌‍‌​

Authoritative narrative: `../../SKILL.md` (Phase 0, Book Architecture, QA Checklist). This is the
ordered execution contract both agents follow; each phase ends at the named gate. The standalone
gate phrase for OV-TRAVEL is **"Type Proceed."**

1. **Phase 0 — Title analysis & auto-configuration.** Title lock + language; trademark/marketplace
   clearance (CLEAR/CAUTION/HIGH RISK); Amazon competitor benchmark (min 10, mean+median, target
   near median within budget); edition-year + season-year band; author identity from
   fakenamegenerator.com; 3-5 subtitle candidates; auto-config (body size, line spacing, two-accent
   palette, cover concept, 12-function chapter map, image + multi-map program, page ledger, trim
   choice, uniqueness fingerprint). Stamp the canonical classification line into `book_lock.md`
   with `framework=AIRF 2.0 Travel Edition`. → **Gate 1 (Configuration)**
2. **Front matter (unnumbered).** Interior title page; copyright + full disclaimers; preface (1 p);
   How To Use This Guide (1 p); live auto-TOC; next-page section break; Introduction full-bleed
   opener; numbering restarts at 1 at Introduction. → **Gate 2 (Front Matter & TOC)**
3. **Introduction & orientation.** Introduction and What To Expect (single-column); orientation
   maps + district overview (IMG-MAP-OVERVIEW).
4. **Main content — 12 functional chapters (repeat per chapter/district).** Full-bleed opener
   (IMG-OP-<SECTION>); two-entry-per-page double-column venue entries in the eleven-point template,
   entry containment, 80-90% fill; one dedicated district map per district chapter
   (IMG-MAP-<DISTRICT>); 3/7/14-day itineraries; section-divider pages at major transitions;
   volatile facts banded "as of [season year]". → **Gate 3 (Main Content, all 12 functions)**
5. **Conclusion.** Approximately 1.5 pages; warm farewell; honest-review request; no new major facts.
6. **Appendix.** Destination-appropriate add-ons selected (packing, budget planner, phrase cards,
   conversion tables, QR/map index, seasonal/tide/ferry planner as the destination requires).
7. **Release QA.** Whole-book page render within budget; continuous numbering from Introduction;
   live TOC resolves; every volatile fact dated and re-verified; all maps original + proofed; every
   QR scanned from final PDF; content-standards + em-dash sweeps; one rolling master DOCX.
   → **Gate 4 (Release)**

Atomic production/resume unit = one of the 12 functional chapters (Phase 4) or one named front/back-matter
section. Production resumes at `next_unfinished_item`; it never re-runs Phase 0 (classification, title
lock, palette, and configuration are locked in `book_lock.md`).
