## A+ Content — generate the 5 modules​‍‍​‌‍‌‌‍‍‌‌‍‌‌‌‍‍‍‍‍‌‍‍‍‌‍‌‌‌‌‍‌‌‍‌​

After uapf-cover-reference (step 5b), produce **five Amazon A+ Content modules** that read
as one system with the cover, using the same shared market pattern (palette, imagery type,
typographic mood) taken from the three best-BSR competitor covers and `cover_prompt.md`.
All module images are generated in **ChatGPT Image 2.0 (gpt-image-2)** — the sole
generator for covers and A+, never Google Flow — sized to Amazon A+ specs, and inserted;
the words live in the module's text fields, not baked into the image. Keep this order and
adapt the wording to the book:

1. **Hero header** — full-width header image (970 x 300) evoking the book's subject in the
   cover's palette, plus a one-line headline stating the book's core promise.
2. **What's inside / key benefit** — standard image + text: a representative subject or
   interior photo (300 x 300 or 970 x 300) beside 2 to 3 sentences on the main benefit and
   who the book is for.
3. **Features / what you'll learn** — four-image-and-text (or highlights) module: four
   short benefit points, each with a small supporting image (300 x 300), covering the
   book's key sections or outcomes.
4. **Why this book** — comparison-style module: a simple chart or paired layout contrasting
   this book's approach with typical alternatives on 3 to 4 honest, unquantified attributes
   (depth, clarity, images/maps, practicality). No competitor names, no prices.
5. **Closing / look inside** — image + text: a sample spread or evocative subject image
   with a short closing line and a soft call to action ("Start [doing X] today").

**A+ copy and image rules:** benefit-led, honest, and unquantified; no prices, shipping,
contact info, time-sensitive claims, external links, or competitor brands (Amazon A+
policy). Images are generated via ChatGPT Image 2.0, text-free or minimal, in the cover
palette, culturally accurate, free of logos, watermarks, and identifiable faces where the
content rules require, and subject to the content restrictions and No-Two-Books-Alike.
Save each module's image plus a short copy doc as a prepared A+ handoff; do not upload.
