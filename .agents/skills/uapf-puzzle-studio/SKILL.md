# UAPF Puzzle Studio (deterministic puzzle builder)​‍‍​‌‍‌‌‍‍‌‌‍‌‌‌‍‍‍‍‍‌‍‍‍‌‍‌‌‌‌‍‌‌‍‌​

uapf-niche-activity (OV-ACT) governs the ARCHITECTURE of puzzle books
(page maps, difficulty arcs, audience routing, count promises). This
studio is its BUILDER: it turns the locked architecture into real,
solvable, verified puzzles. Puzzles are GENERATED BY CODE, never
free-written by the model: a hand-written grid cannot be trusted to be
solvable, and global rule 7 (no fabrication) applies to solvability
claims.

## Word search (core builder)

```
python .agents/skills/uapf-puzzle-studio/word_search_gen.py build <spec.json>
```

1. GENIE WRITES THE SPEC: per-puzzle theme + word list (the creative
   layer: themes follow the book concept; words are real, spellable,
   audience-appropriate, and respect the global content restrictions).
   Audience presets: kids (11x11 grid, right/down only, 16pt),
   adults (15x15, all 8 directions, 13pt), seniors (15x15, no reversed
   words, 16pt: the senior type floor applied to grids).
2. THE GENERATOR BUILDS: places every word or FAILS the build loudly
   (a word that cannot be placed is reported, never silently dropped:
   count promises are sacred). Fill letters are random; a fixed `seed`
   makes every build reproducible.
3. OUTPUT: puzzle pages (numbered, themed, block-letter headings), a
   SOLUTIONS section with the answer letters bolded in place, and a
   machine-readable `_answers.json` ledger (the QA Test-Solver's input).
4. VERIFY: after generation, re-derive each placement from the ledger
   and confirm the grid letters spell the word (the build script's demo
   flow shows the pattern). A book ships only after 100% of placements
   verify. Then render QA (rule 17) checks the printed page: grids
   inside margins, no clipped rows, folios styled.

## Difficulty arc

The OV-ACT progressive-difficulty law maps to generator knobs: early
puzzles use fewer, shorter words and no diagonals; later puzzles add
diagonals, reverses (not for seniors), and denser grids. Record the arc
in the book contract and reflect it honestly in the subtitle ("Easy to
Challenging"), never as a cognitive-benefit claim.

## Language law

Word searches are LANGUAGE-DEPENDENT (OV-ACT classification): a
localized edition is rebuilt from a locale-specific word list, never
mechanically translated. The generator handles A-Z; accented or
non-Latin editions need a locale alphabet agreed with the operator
before the build.

## Variants in scope

- WORD SCRAMBLE: shuffle the theme words (code, with a check that the
  scramble differs from the answer), render two-column with answers in
  back.
- SIMPLE SUBSTITUTION CIPHERS (cryptogram-lite for kids/gift books):
  generated mapping, answer key mandatory.
- Sudoku, kakuro, crosswords, and mazes are NOT yet code-backed in this
  studio: if a brief needs them, say so honestly and scope the generator
  work with the operator first; never hand-write those puzzle bodies.

## Book assembly and tiers

The studio's DOCX is the puzzle BLOCK; the full book (front matter,
intro, progress tracker, notes) assembles through the standard OV-ACT
pipeline with the premium interior system (tinted theme headers, styled
folios) and passes uapf-editorial-pass on its prose sections only
(puzzle bodies are exempt from rhythm checks by design). Interior
references come from uapf-interior-db (`interior_db.py pick --niche
puzzle`).
Included in every Community Edition install. Binds BOTH engines, Claude
and Codex alike.
