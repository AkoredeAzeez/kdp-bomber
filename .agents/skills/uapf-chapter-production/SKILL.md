---
name: uapf-chapter-production
description: Research, draft, review, correct, and merge one UAPF chapter at a time while keeping internal source IDs out of reader-facing text.
---

1. Read the chapter brief, research packet, source and claim ledgers, prior
   chapters, terminology, style/fact ledger, page target, and relevant overlay.
2. Block drafting only for that chapter when material evidence is missing; queue
   the blocker and continue independent work.
3. Draft substantive, audience-specific prose that fulfills the chapter brief.
4. Keep the tone professional, specific, natural, and non-mechanical.
5. Keep source-to-claim traceability in the internal claim ledger rather than raw
   inline `SRC-####` markers.
6. Remove any raw source ID from reader-facing prose, tables, captions, callouts,
   footnotes, and endnotes.
6b. Format the chapter per the GLOBAL premium interior mandate in AGENTS.md
    ("Manuscript formatting — premium interior"): styled opener, colored
    typographic system, tinted callout panels, styled tables, shaded unit
    header bars — in the book's fingerprint palette, grayscale-legible.
    Plain black-on-white chapters fail QA in every niche.
6c. Keep every image and table inside the page margins, caption each image
    directly beneath it as "Figure C.N: description", and label each table
    directly beneath it as "Table C.N", numbered per chapter and reset each
    chapter.
7. Run QA, proofreading, consistency, policy, risk, originality, overlay,
   page-band, premium-interior, and source-ID checks.
8. Correct failures and rerun the checks.
9. Write the completion report and rebuild the rolling DOCX.
10. CHAPTER PREVIEW DELIVERY (operator directive 2026-08-14): before moving to
    the next chapter, render the completed chapter and deliver it to the
    operator as BOTH a DOCX and a PDF download (SendUserFile or clickable file
    links) so any issue can be spotted immediately. The preview must show the
    chapter exactly as it will ship: full premium color design (styled opener,
    colored typography, tinted panels, styled tables, header bars) in the
    book's fingerprint palette; a plain or unstyled preview is a failure.
    Under AUTO-ADVANCE, deliver both files and continue to the next chapter
    without waiting; otherwise wait per the normal gate.
